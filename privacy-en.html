Privacy Policy – Gold Wallet

Last Updated: June 2026

Gold Wallet respects your privacy and is designed with a local-first approach. Most of your information remains stored on your device unless you choose to enable cloud backup.

Information Stored on Your Device

  • Your portfolio data.
  • Gold items, weights, and purchase information.
  • Application settings.
  • Photos attached to your items.
  • Locally generated reports.

Cloud Backup (Optional)

If you enable Cloud Backup, the app creates an encrypted backup of your data.

  • On Android, backups are stored in your own Google Drive App Data storage.
  • On Apple devices, backups are stored in your own iCloud account.
  • We do not store backups on developer-owned servers.
  • We cannot access, view, or restore your backup files.

Authentication

The app may use Google Sign-In or Sign in with Apple only to connect to your personal cloud backup storage.

Analytics and Notifications

The app may use Firebase Analytics, Firebase Cloud Messaging, and Firebase In-App Messaging to improve the app experience, deliver notifications, and show important in-app messages. These services do not include the contents of your portfolio or backup files.

Advertising

The free version may display ads through Google AdMob. Google may use advertising identifiers according to its own privacy policy. On Apple devices, the app may request permission through App Tracking Transparency before personalized ads are enabled.

In-App Purchases

If you purchase premium features or remove ads, payments are processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We do not collect or store your payment information.

Third-Party Services

  • Google AdMob
  • Firebase Analytics
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging
  • Firebase In-App Messaging
  • Google Sign-In
  • Google Drive App Data
  • Sign in with Apple
  • Apple iCloud

Your Choices

  • You may use the app offline.
  • You may enable or disable cloud backup.
  • You may delete your cloud backup.
  • You may sign out of your cloud account.
  • You may delete local app data from your device.

Disclaimer
